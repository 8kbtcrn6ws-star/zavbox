About

Zavbox is Zav's game library for the TV. A phone acts as the controller for this menu: D-pad to move, A to choose, B to go back, Home to return here.

Pick a game on the shelf and press Play. It opens on this TV and shows its own QR code: scan that with your phone to play. This Zavbox controller steps aside once the game starts.

A future game can only join this library if it can be launched from the TV and then played with a phone in the same living-room way.
